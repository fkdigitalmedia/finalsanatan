# SanatanTools — High-Authority Organic Backlink & Digital PR Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Objective

This strategy document outlines the 100% white-hat, high-authority backlink acquisition and digital distribution framework for **SanatanTools** (`https://www.sanatantools.com`). ## 3. Outreach & Acquisition Campaigns

### Campaign 1: Academic & University Indology Outreach (.edu / .ac.in)
- **Target**: Departments of Sanskrit, Indic Studies, Asian Linguistics, and Astronomy at universities (e.g., BHU, Sampurnanand Sanskrit Vishwavidyalaya, Oxford Centre for Hindu Studies, Columbia, Heidelberg).
- **Pitch**: Provide access to open-source Sanskrit computational tools (Sandhi Splitter, Dhatu Conjugator, Shloka Meter Analyzer) as free academic research aids for curriculum resource pages.
- **Anchor Texts**: *Sanskrit computational tools*, *Devanagari verb conjugator*, *Sanskrit dictionary and thesaurus*.

### Campaign 2: Temple Trusts & Religious Directory Inclusion
- **Target**: Official websites of major temple trusts (Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams, Somnath Trust, Kashi Vishwanath, Vaishno Devi) and regional cultural associations (Bhartiya Vidya Bhavan, Chinmaya Mission, ISKCON).
- **Pitch**: Listing in resource directories for daily Panchang, Tithi calculators, and festival calendars.
- **Anchor Texts**: *Daily Hindu Panchang*, *Festival calendar and muhurat timings*.

### Campaign 3: Embeddable Calculator & Panchang Widgets
- **Target**: Regional news portals, community blogs, devotional bloggers, wedding planners.
- **Mechanism**: Provide responsive, lightweight iframe widgets (e.g. "Today's Choghadiya", "Daily Shloka of the Day", "Kundli Matching Widget") that include a natural canonical attribution link to `https://www.sanatantools.com`.
- **Value**: Passive backlink generation as hundreds of lifestyle and regional blogs embed the widgets for their audience.

### Campaign 4: Digital PR & Major Festival Data Releases
- **Target**: National news outlets (Times of India, Hindustan Times, Dainik Bhaskar, The Hindu), lifestyle editors, and journalists covering religious festivities.
- **Hook**: Quarterly data-driven reports on astronomical alignments for major festivals (Maha Shivaratri, Diwali, Makar Sankranti, Solar/Lunar Eclipses, Chhath Puja).
- **Value**: High-authority editorial citations (`.com`, `.in`, `.org`) in national news articles.

## 4. Internal Link Architecture & PageRank Distribution

To maximize link equity passing from external backlinks to deep tool pages:
1. **Pillar Pages**: The root (`/`), `/kundli`, `/panchang`, `/astrology`, `/sanskrit`, and `/festivals` serve as primary equity hubs.
2. **Dynamic Contextual Links**: Every tool and programmatic landing page uses `src/lib/seo/internal-links.ts` to surface 4–8 relevant sibling pages with descriptive, keyword-rich anchor text.
3. **Breadcrumb Silos**: Full JSON-LD `BreadcrumbList` microdata on all pages enforces category hierarchy (`Home > Category > Tool`).

---

## 5. White-Hat Compliance & Safety Standards

- **Zero Link Schemes**: No private blog networks (PBNs), automated link-spamming software, or paid dofollow link insertions.
- **Google Search Essentials Adherence**: All external links generated from user profiles or reviews carry `rel="nofollow ugc"`.
- **Anchor Text Diversity**: Maintain a balanced anchor distribution (40% branded "SanatanTools", 35% descriptive/partial match, 15% URL, 10% generic).
