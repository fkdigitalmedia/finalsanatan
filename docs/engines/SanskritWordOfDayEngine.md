# Sanskrit Word of the Day & Lexicon Engine Documentation

## 1. Overview
The **Sanskrit Word of the Day Engine** (`src/components/tools/word-of-day/word-engine.ts`) delivers an authentic daily Sanskrit lexicon and philosophical contemplation experience with deep etymological derivation, canonical scriptural quotations, and digital sharing tools.

---

## 2. Data Structure & Corpus

Each word entry includes:
- **Devanagari & IAST**: Authentic representation in both scripts.
- **Etymology (व्युत्पत्ति)**: Dhatu root, prefix (उपसर्ग), and suffix (प्रत्यय) breakdown according to Paninian Nirukta traditions.
- **Gender (लिङ्ग)**: पुंल्लिङ्गम्, स्त्रीलिङ्गम्, or नपुंसकलिंगम् classification.
- **Scriptural Citation (शास्त्र प्रमाण)**: Verse citation from Upanishads (मुण्डक, तैत्तिरीय, श्वेताश्वतर), Bhagavad Gita, and Smritis.
- **Synonyms & Antonyms (पर्यायवाची व विलोम)**: Classical Sanskrit vocabulary relations.
- **Spiritual Reflection (साधना विचार)**: Practical Vedantic and yogic contemplation.

---

## 3. UI Features & Export
- **Deterministic & Random Discovery**: Deterministic calculation based on calendar day or random discovery shuffle.
- **Audio Chanting / Pronunciation**: Speech synthesis for accurate Sanskrit diction.
- **1200x700px Wisdom Card Generator**: Generates styled PNG cards directly in the browser via HTML5 Canvas for WhatsApp and social media status sharing.
