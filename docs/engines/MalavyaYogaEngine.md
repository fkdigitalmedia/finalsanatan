# Malavya Yoga Calculator & Analysis Engine Documentation

## 1. Overview
The **Malavya Yoga Calculator & Analysis Engine** (`src/components/tools/yogas/malavya-engine.ts`) provides full Vedic calculation and evaluation for **Malavya Yoga (मालव्य योग)**, one of the supreme **Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas (पञ्च महापुरुष योग)** formed by **Venus (शुक्र देव)**.

---

## 2. Astrological Rules of Formation (पाराशरीय सूत्र)
According to *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS)* and *Phaladeepika*:
$$\text{Venus} \in \{\text{Taurus (वृषभ)}, \text{Libra (तुला)}, \text{Pisces (मीन)}\} \quad \text{AND} \quad \text{House}(\text{Venus}) \in \{1, 4, 7, 10\}$$

### 2.1 Dignity Matrix
- **Exalted (उच्च)**: Pisces ($27^\circ$ deep exaltation)
- **Moolatrikona / Own Sign (स्वराशि)**: Libra ($0^\circ - 15^\circ$ Moolatrikona, $15^\circ - 30^\circ$ Swakshetra) and Taurus
- **Debilitated (नीच)**: Virgo ($27^\circ$ deep debilitation — Cancels Malavya Yoga unless Neechabhanga is formed).

### 2.2 Modifiers & Strength Scoring
- **Combustion (अस्त)**: If Venus is within $10^\circ$ of the Sun, the yoga is rendered partial/combust, reducing the material manifest score by $30\%$.
- **Retrograde (वक्री / चेष्टा बल)**: Retrograde Venus receives maximum *Cheshta Bala*, boosting yoga efficacy.
- **Aspects**: Benefic aspect from Jupiter further fortifies artistic and royal renown.

---

## 3. House-Specific Manifestations
- **1st House (Lagna / Tanu Bhava)**: Extraordinary personal beauty, charismatic aura, leadership, and spontaneous public love.
- **4th House (Sukha Bhava)**: Luxurious real estate, palatial mansions, high-end motor vehicles, and emotional contentment.
- **7th House (Kalatra Bhava)**: Refined, wealthy, and cultured spouse, thriving international commerce and diplomatic prowess.
- **10th House (Karma Bhava)**: Apex status in cinema, arts, fashion, gastronomy, architecture, diplomacy, or judicial administration.

## 4. Vedic Remedies & Activation
- **Mantra**: *ॐ शुं शुक्राय नमः* / *ॐ द्रां द्रीं द्रौं सः शुक्राय नमः* (108 repetitions on Fridays).
- **Gemstones**: Diamond (हीरा), White Sapphire (श्वेत पुखराज), or Australian Opal.
- **Rudraksha**: 6-Mukhi Rudraksha (Kartikeya / Venus energy).
- **Worship**: Recitation of *Sri Suktam* and *Kanakadhara Stotram*.
- **Charity (दान)**: Rice, milk, white sweets, and white clothing on Fridays.
