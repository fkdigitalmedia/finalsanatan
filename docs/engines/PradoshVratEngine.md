# Pradosha Vrat & Shiva Mahatmya Engine Documentation

## 1. Overview
The **Pradosha Vrat & Shiva Mahatmya Engine** (`src/components/tools/pradosh/pradosh-engine.ts`) delivers an astronomical calculation engine for determining **Pradosha Kaal (प्रदोष काल)** windows, Trayodashi Pradosh-vyapini dates, weekday-specific spiritual classifications (*Som, Bhauma, Shani Pradosh*), 8-step Shodashopachara Shiva Puja Vidhi, and sacred Shiva Stotras.

---

## 2. Astronomical Calculation Principles

### 2.1 Pradosha Kaal Muhurta
- **Window Definition**: The sacred twilight period spanning **2 to 3 Ghatis (48 to 72 minutes) or 6 Ghatis (2 hours 24 minutes)** beginning precisely at astronomical sunset (*सूर्यास्त*).
- **Location Awareness**: Calculates local sunset dynamically based on geographical latitude, longitude, and elevation.

### 2.2 Trayodashi Pradosh-Vyapini Rule
- Follows canonical *Nirnayasindhu* and *Dharmasindhu* principles where the evening containing Trayodashi Tithi during Pradosha Kaal is recognized as the authentic Pradosha Vrat day.
- Covers both **Shukla Paksha Trayodashi** and **Krishna Paksha Trayodashi** across any Gregorian year.

## 3. Weekday Classifications & Planetary Remedies
- **Som Pradosh (Monday / Moon)**: Mental peace, fulfillment of desires, and Chandra Dosha alleviation.
- **Bhauma Pradosh (Tuesday / Mars)**: Debt clearance (*ऋण मुक्ति*), courage, and Mangal Dosha pacification.
- **Budha Pradosh (Wednesday / Mercury)**: Intellect, commerce, education, and child happiness.
- **Guru Pradosh (Thursday / Jupiter)**: Pitra Dosha relief, spiritual knowledge, and auspicious marriage prospects.
- **Shukra Pradosh (Friday / Venus)**: Wealth, marital harmony, and aesthetic bliss.
- **Shani Pradosh (Saturday / Saturn)**: Alleviation of Sade Sati & Dhaiya, longevity, and liberation.
- **Ravi Pradosh (Sunday / Sun)**: Supreme health, vitality, leadership, and Surya Dosha purification.

## 4. Puja Vidhi & Sacred Chants
- **8-Step Ritual Guide**: Morning purification, Sankalpa, sunset lamp lighting, Panchamrita Abhishekam, Bilva leaf offering, Vrat Katha recitation, Rudrashtakam chanting, Aarti, and next-day sunrise Parana.
- **Stotras & Mantras**: Complete *Shri Rudrashtakam* and *Shiva Panchakshara Stotram* with word-by-word meaning and audio chanting.
- **Calendar Integration**: 1-Click `.ics` calendar sync for Google Calendar & Apple Calendar.
